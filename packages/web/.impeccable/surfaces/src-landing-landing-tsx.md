# Surface brief: Slabs landing page (route: signed-out visitors)

Scope and mode: Persuade, inside the established world (DESIGN.md "The Quiet Workbench": shadcn/ui, Geist, one blue). Code-led; the owner confirmed structure and facts in docs/briefs/landing-and-private-projects.md.

Audience: DIY homeowners cladding attic rooms and carpenters. Job: decide to sign in (Google) and try it. Action: "Zaloguj się przez Google" (Clerk modal). Proof: the real engine moving a real wall; real app drawings. No testimonials, counts, ratings, prices beyond "Bezpłatnie". PL + EN, WCAG 2.2 AA, 390 px, landing chunk must not load three.js, jsPDF or the editor.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the product doing its one job, live: move one start point and the plate count and waste change. It refuses the standard hero (headline beside a device mockup) and the row of equal feature cards.
OWN-WORLD: The app's workshop-drawing world scaled up: white paper cards on a light grey ground, hairline borders, one blue that is chalk joint lines and every action, pencil X on slivers, Geist with tabular numerals; drawings are the only imagery. One committed blue field closes the page.
STORY: The visitor sees that measurements become a plate count and a cutting plan, believes it because their own drag changed real engine numbers, and signs in with Google (free, Bezpłatnie).
FIRST VIEWPORT: Top row: Slabs wordmark left, language switch right. Below, 5/12 left: display headline (about 4rem, 600), one supporting sentence, the primary button "Zaloguj się przez Google" with "Bezpłatnie" beside it. 7/12 right: the live wall card, the real roof-slope drawing about 600 px wide with a draggable R, the plate count and waste at 3rem beside the drawing, caption "Przykład na żywo, nic się nie zapisuje". On a phone the wall sits directly under the button.
FORM: Live sample wall, the owner's chosen structure (seed: none (pinned): "Live sample wall" in the owner's brief).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
